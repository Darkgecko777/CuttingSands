# UI layout

How the play frame is divided, and what each part has to tell the player. This is the document for composition. The look of the pieces is `docs/UI_Visuals.md`.

The words on the controls are a string table. They will change. This document names roles. Naming a tag here does not add a role, rename one, or decide what a feature is.

The live shell still wears its current plates. This document is the frame outside pictures are drawn for. Those pictures reach the shell in a later pass. This checkout does not keep a side scene to preview them.

## One frame

After the patron is chosen, play is one frame. It does not open a second full-screen place. A new feature is another tag on the book, or more work inside a desk that already exists.

From top to bottom:

1. **Ground** — a full-bleed picture of the stop. On the road, a picture of the road.
2. **Top banner** — always present. Worn paper over the ground.
3. **Ledger** — the page block, the full width under the top banner. A solid binding is on the left.
4. **Scrolls** — hanging from the rod. One open at a time, and each can close. Opening one fills the well.
5. **Well** — the open gap under the rod, down to the bottom banner. No frame of its own. The ground shows through. The open scroll's panel fills this gap.
6. **Bottom banner** — worn paper. The desks, while stopped.

At a glance: where you are and the state of the wagon, which tag is open, then which desk you are at.

## Ground

Each stop has its own picture. The road has its own picture. The picture fills the frame, behind every banner and behind the well.

While moving, with no tag open, the road picture is the frame. Opening a tag pauses the journey. Closing it resumes.

## Top banner

- A small mark for the patron you serve.
- Where you are. While moving, where you are bound. This name is the largest type in the frame.
- The clock.
- One purse.
- How full the wagon is, by count and by weight.
- One mark for the sky on the road that matters. While stopped the mark is blank. While moving it shows that road's term from the closed list. The banner does not invent conditions, and it does not show a second place's sky beside the first.
- One system control, a round seal. It opens the same pause from every screen.

The name does not carry a second line. The open desk is the selected seal on the bottom banner.

## Tags

Each tag opens one body of work in the well and closes the others. Closing the open tag returns the well to the ground.

The row covers what you carry, the chart, and what you have heard. The person, and the eyes you have posted, are named so the row can be seen whole. They join play when they are built. So does any later feature of that kind. A new tag does not get its own band, a side column, or a destination screen.

What you have heard is a list in its own tag. Those entries are not marks on the chart.

## Well

The well is a space. It has no border, no plate, and no sheet of its own. The ground is the field.

Only these arrangements, set inside that space:

- **Two regions.** The work, and a note on the thing selected. Neither region has a frame. The note explains. It does not keep a private set of actions. Actions belong to the current desk, or to the tag when the tag itself is the work.
- **One picture, the chart.** The project's plate, letterboxed. Routes on that plate stay as drawn. The plate is supplied from outside these documents. That chart does not hide what you have already learned.
- **One decision card** over the center: a title, a short passage, a way through. Used for a single beat. It is not a new arrangement of the frame, and it is not a second well.

Nothing else sits beside the well. A sketch that adds a column, a floating shop, or a full-screen room is a picture, not a layout change.

## Bottom banner

Drawn while stopped. Dead while moving. You do not take the road from the chart.

Three desks. A stop does not always draw all three. Each is a seal.

- **House desk.** Only where a house receives you. Cities, and Ghorath. Obligations owed to houses, standing, and at your own seat a private room. Time does not pass here.
- **Stall.** Every stop. Exchange only. Closed for part of the day. Time does not pass here, so the stall cannot open because the clock was allowed to slip.
- **Leaving yard.** Every stop. The only place time is passed on purpose. The only place you refill for the road. The only place you take the road. The home of any once-a-day conversation, and of any short job that starts at this stop.

One desk is current. The start of a session, and every arrival, land on the stall with no tag open.

A line of work on a desk is a seal inside the well, on that desk's screen. It is not a new tag, and it is not a control painted onto the chart.

## What a new feature may change

- One more tag on the book.
- What the well shows for that tag.
- What the note is about.

## What stays put

- One frame, one purse, one wagon.
- The top banner's jobs.
- The three desk roles, and which stops draw them.
- The chart as the project's plate, inside the well.
- Heard things kept off the chart.
- Time passing only in the leaving yard.
